Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account we collect your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form). As you use the service we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, to record and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. You may ask us to delete your account and all associated expenses at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data removed, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided on the site.
